Generate the moved blocks instead of typing them, for example:
for b in a b c d e f; do for r in 1 2 3 4 5; do for s in 01 02 03 04 05 06 07 08 09 10; do printf 'moved {\n  from = local_file.seat_%s_%s_%s\n  to   = local_file.seat["%s-%s-%s"]\n}\n\n' $b $r $s $b $r $s; done; done; done > moved.tf
Run `terraform plan`: it should say 300 to move and 0 to destroy.
